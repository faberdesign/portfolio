About

Moin, I’m Benedikt.
Nice to meet you!

I’m a Concept Creative with several years of experience in live communication: live events, digtal and hybrid formats, 360° campaigns. I translate brand values and messages into experiences that stay with people, with interactions that feel intuitive and moments that leave an impression.

In practice, that means deciding what people see first, what they feel next, and where the story needs to hold still or turn.

When I’m not developing ideas or down some AI rabbit hole, I'm wandering around Hamburg with one of my analogue cameras.
